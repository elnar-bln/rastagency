2. Our services

RAST is a performance marketing agency. Our main service is pay-per-click (PPC) advertising: planning, setting up, managing and optimizing paid campaigns on platforms such as Google Ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and LinkedIn. Our campaigns focus on reaching high-intent users, meaning people whose searches or behaviour show they are close to a purchase or enquiry.

Depending on the agreed scope, services may also include conversion tracking setup, remarketing, product feed management, reporting and landing page recommendations. The exact scope, channels, deliverables and fees for each client are set out in a written proposal or agreement.

Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately and starts only after you confirm it in writing (email is enough).

3. Advertising budget

  • Advertising spend (the money paid to Google, Meta, LinkedIn or other platforms for clicks and impressions) is separate from our management fee.
  • Ad spend is normally paid by you directly to the platform, using your own payment method in your own ad account. If we agree that RAST pays the platform on your behalf, you must pay that amount to us in advance.
  • We manage campaigns within the budget you approve and will not increase spend above it without your written approval.
  • Platforms set their own prices, policies and billing rules, including how they handle invalid clicks. RAST does not control these and is not responsible for charges made by a platform.

Performance depends on factors outside our control, including competition, seasonality, your prices and offer, your website, and changes in platform algorithms and policies.

Any forecasts or estimates we share are our best judgement at the time and are not promises.
